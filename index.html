CHAPTER TWO — A NOTE FROM ME

Something I wanted you to know.

Dear Aurum,

If this were a movie, I don't think I could ever write a character quite like you. Not because you're perfect, but because being exactly yourself is already my favourite thing.

You make the ordinary feel a little brighter just by being part of it. Your kindness, your heart, your wonderfully-you way of seeing the world: I notice it all, and I love you for it.

Thank you for making room for laughter, for showing up as yourself, and for making love feel both like a grand adventure and the safest place to land.

I hope you know that you are appreciated in all the quiet moments, not just the big ones. I am so glad this story brought our pages together.

And, for the record, if I had to choose my favourite leading man, I wouldn't even need a second take.

With all my love,

Always yours A
